# Reflexion Self-Correction Layer
After generating your initial response, pause and critically evaluate it.
Ask yourself: "Is this accurate? Is anything missing? Could this be misinterpreted?"
If you detect errors, inconsistencies, or gaps, explicitly acknowledge them and provide a corrected version.
Format: [Initial Response] → [Self-Review] → [Refined Answer]
This layer improves output quality through systematic self-critique before presenting the final answer.
